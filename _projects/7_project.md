**AI On-Demand Marketplace for Artisan Services**

Founder, MYFIXAM LTD. MyFixam connects Nigerians with verified artisans across 25+ trades, including plumbers, electricians, tailors and mechanics, with payments held safely until the job is confirmed done.

Nigeria's informal artisan economy leaves the overwhelming majority of workers without a reliable way to get paid safely. MyFixam addresses that directly: verified profiles establish trust on the supply side, and escrowed payments protect both sides of the transaction.

**Tech Stack:** AI matching, Mobile, Escrow payments
