**AI-Powered Community Early Warning System**

Tsaro is a community security and early warning system built for northern Nigeria, where the people most at risk are often the least likely to have reliable connectivity.

End-to-end system with a Flask backend and a React Native frontend, combining fine-tuned Gemma models with SMS-tier delivery so alerts still reach communities on basic handsets and patchy networks.

Built for the Kaggle Gemma 4 Good Hackathon.

**Tech Stack:** Gemma (fine-tuned), Flask, React Native, SMS delivery
